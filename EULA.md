## 6. Local Data and Privacy

CUE SAMPLER processes audio on your device. To restore your work and support playback, analysis, and stem separation, it may store audio, source-file paths, analysis results, edit settings, and audio-derived cache keys in project files and local caches. Your DAW, operating system, or backup service may separately manage copies of those files.

The former optional CUE data-sharing feature has been removed. Current CUE application code does not upload your loaded audio, source-file paths, audio hashes, detection results, corrections, or usage events to CUE. Starting with version 1.0.9, CUE explicitly requests that its bundled ONNX Runtime disable diagnostic telemetry before model sessions are created. Your host application and operating system have their own independent privacy practices.

The Software automatically checks GitHub Releases for updates, subject to a daily interval, and supports manual checks. Requests include ordinary connection information, including your IP address and a generic application User-Agent, but no audio, filenames, analysis results, or installation identifier. Update results and preferences are cached locally. GitHub handles connection information under its own privacy statement.

Our Privacy Policy covers the Site, purchases, communications, support, and the removal of the former data-sharing feature: https://www.cuesampler.com/landing/embed#privacy. You can also open Privacy from the navigation at https://www.cuesampler.com.
