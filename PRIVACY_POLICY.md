PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CUE ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit cuesampler.com (the "Site"), subscribe to our newsletter, or purchase and use the CUE Sampler VST plugin (the "Software").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you interact with our Site, purchase our Software, and—only if you choose to enable optional data sharing—when you use the Software.

Identifiers & Contact Information: Name, email address, billing address, and phone number.

Payment & Financial Data: When you purchase the CUE Sampler VST plugin, your payment details (such as credit card numbers or account info) are processed. Note: We do not store or process your full financial details on our servers. All payments are securely handled by our third-party payment processors.

Device & Usage Data (Website): IP address, browser type, operating system, and information about how you interact with our website (collected via cookies and tracking technologies).

In-Software Usage Data (Optional): The Software includes an optional data-sharing feature that is turned OFF by default and is described in Section 2 below.

2. In-Software (Plugin) Data Collection — Optional, Off by Default

The CUE Sampler plugin includes an optional data-sharing feature ("Data Sharing") that is turned OFF by default. No usage data is collected from or transmitted by the plugin unless you affirmatively enable Data Sharing using the in-plugin toggle. You can turn it off again at any time, which stops all further collection and transmission.

What is collected when (and only when) you enable Data Sharing:

A randomly generated installation identifier (a one-time random ID stored locally that does not contain your name, email, or any account information);

A one-way hash (an irreversible fingerprint) of audio samples you load into the plugin — we cannot reconstruct, listen to, or recover your audio from this value, and we do not collect or transmit your actual audio;

Technical characteristics of loaded samples: duration, sample rate, and number of channels;

Tempo (BPM) detection results and any manual corrections you make to them;

Musical key detection results and any manual corrections you make to them;

Timestamps for the above events.

Why we collect it: solely to measure and improve the accuracy of the plugin's automatic BPM and key detection. This data is technical and product-improvement oriented; it is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Where it goes: when enabled, these events are sent securely to our data-collection endpoint hosted on Google's infrastructure (Google Apps Script / Google Workspace), which acts as a service provider/processor on our behalf. We do not share this data with advertisers or other third parties.

Your control: Data Sharing is opt-in. Because the data is keyed to a random installation identifier rather than your identity, in most cases we are unable to link it back to you personally. If you have enabled Data Sharing and wish to request deletion of data associated with your installation identifier, contact us at cue@cuesampler.com and provide your installation identifier (viewable in the plugin), and we will delete the associated records where we are able to identify them.

To Improve the Site and Product: Analyzing website traffic trends for optimization, and—where you have enabled Data Sharing—analyzing anonymous detection data to improve the accuracy of the Software's BPM and key detection.

Security & Fraud Prevention: Verifying transactions, protecting against unauthorized digital distribution, and complying with legal obligations.

Web Hosting & Analytics Providers: To maintain our website infrastructure and monitor site performance.

Product Analytics Provider (Google): If you enable the optional in-Software Data Sharing feature, the anonymous usage data described in Section 2 is processed and stored using Google's infrastructure (Google Apps Script / Google Workspace) on our behalf.

We may also disclose your information if required to do so by law, to enforce our terms of sale, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of CUE and its users.
