## 1. Purchases, Support, and Communications

We receive information you provide or that our business providers make available for order fulfillment and support, such as your name, email, order details, and the content of your messages. Provide only information needed for your request. If you voluntarily send audio, project files, or diagnostic information to support, we receive and use those files to investigate the issue.

Gumroad operates the storefront and checkout and acts as merchant of record for Gumroad purchases. It processes payment and billing information and provides order and delivery services under its own terms and privacy policy: https://gumroad.com/privacy. CUE does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details through that checkout.

We use order information to deliver purchases, assist with downloads, and handle support or refund requests. We use contact information to answer you and send relevant transaction or service messages. Marketing subscriptions are optional, and you can unsubscribe using the link in those messages or contact us. We do not treat a purchase as blanket permission for unrelated marketing.

## 2. CUE SAMPLER: Local Processing and Update Checks

CUE SAMPLER processes audio on your device. Audio, source-file paths, analysis results, edit settings, and audio-derived cache keys may be stored in project files and local caches to support playback, project restoration, analysis, and stem separation. Local caches and projects are not automatically uploaded to CUE by these features. Your DAW, operating system, and backup or file-sync services may separately manage your files under their own settings and policies.

The former optional CUE data-sharing feature has been removed. Current CUE application code does not upload loaded audio, source-file paths, audio hashes, detection results, corrections, or usage events to CUE. Starting with version 1.0.9, CUE explicitly requests that its bundled ONNX Runtime disable diagnostic telemetry before model sessions are created. This does not control independent diagnostics or data handling by your DAW or operating system.

The Software automatically checks GitHub Releases for updates, subject to a daily interval, and supports manual checks. GitHub receives ordinary connection metadata, including your IP address and a generic application User-Agent. CUE's update request does not include audio, filenames, analysis results, or an installation identifier. Update results and preferences are cached locally. GitHub's handling is described at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.
