PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CUE ("we," "us," or "our") handles personal information when you visit cuesampler.com (the "Site"), subscribe to our newsletter, purchase CUE Sampler, or use the CUE Sampler VST plugin (the "Software").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide when interacting with our Site, purchasing the Software, subscribing to communications, or contacting support.

Identifiers & Contact Information: Name, email address, billing address, phone number, and information included in support requests.

Payment & Financial Data: Payment details are processed by our third-party payment processors. We do not store or process full payment-card or bank-account details on our servers.

Website Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, cookies, and information about how you interact with the Site.

2. CUE Sampler Plugin Privacy

The Software does not include usage telemetry or an in-plugin data-sharing feature. It does not collect, store, or transmit:

- Loaded audio or file names;
- Audio fingerprints or hashes;
- BPM or musical-key detection results or corrections;
- Plugin usage events;
- Random installation or device identifiers.

The Software may make an unauthenticated HTTPS request to GitHub Releases to check whether a newer version is available. The request contains no installation identifier or usage telemetry. Like an ordinary web request, it necessarily exposes network metadata such as an IP address and User-Agent to GitHub, whose handling of that information is governed by GitHub's privacy terms.

3. How We Use Information

We use information collected through the Site and our business providers to:

- Process purchases and deliver Software downloads;
- Manage license keys and send transaction receipts;
- Respond to support requests and communicate about updates;
- Send newsletters where you have subscribed;
- Improve and secure the Site;
- Prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use plugin telemetry because the Software does not collect it.

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell, rent, or lease personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to operate our business, including:

- Payment processors;
- Email marketing and delivery providers;
- Web hosting and website analytics providers;
- Download and software-delivery providers.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of CUE and its users.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Site may use cookies to remember checkout preferences, support website functionality, and analyze traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser, although doing so may limit parts of the Site or checkout experience. These website technologies are separate from the Software, which contains no usage telemetry.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you reside, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection regarding personal information we hold about you. You may unsubscribe from promotional email using the link included in those messages.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information handled through our Site and service providers. No transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site and Software are not directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in the applicable jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so that we can take appropriate action.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Changes will be identified by the "Last Updated" date above.
