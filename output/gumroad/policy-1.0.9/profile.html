Main Features

CUE Sampler — What It Does

Drop a song
Build and refine chops

01

Automatic BPM & Key Detection

Load a supported audio file for automatic tempo and musical-key estimates. Review the results and adjust the tempo, grid, or key when needed.

02

Auto-Chop & Bar Slicing

Use the detected tempo and grid to create chops, then adjust boundaries and cue points to fit the recording.

03

Instant MIDI Mapping

Every chop is laid out across your MIDI keyboard the moment slicing finishes, so you can start playing the arrangement immediately.

04

On-Device AI Stem Separation

Separate vocals, bass, and drums on your device, then chop the results. Analysis runs in the background; processing time varies with the sample and your hardware.

05

Pitch, Time & Playback Control

Real-time pitch-shifting, time-stretching, and tempo warping built on transient-preserving DSP, plus reverse playback and gate envelope shaping per chop.

06

Works In Your DAW

Mac installers include AU and VST3; Windows installers include VST3. Use a compatible 64-bit host. No native AAX version for Pro Tools is supplied.

About CUE Audio Plugins

For Producers, By Producers

Next-Gen Audio Software
VST3 & AU Instruments

CUE engineers high-performance, AI-assisted audio plugins and creative VST3/AU sampling instruments for modern music producers, beatmakers, and audio engineers.

CUE Sampler combines automatic BPM and key estimates, on-device AI stem separation, and MIDI mapping. Load supported audio, review the detected grid, and refine your chops in a compatible AU or VST3 host. Results and processing time depend on the source material and hardware.
